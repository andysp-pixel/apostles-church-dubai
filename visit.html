Use the address and directions above. If you need help when you arrive, call the church on +971 52 437 3611.

Your privacy

This website does not use analytics, advertising cookies or accounts. Prayer drafts stay in this page’s memory and are cleared when you leave or reload. They are not sent or stored by this website. A temporary browser-tab flag remembers that you have seen the welcome animation; it contains no personal information.

A prayer request is shared only when you copy it and choose to send it yourself through the church’s Facebook page. The daily NIV verse connects to Bible Gateway automatically. Any configured donation checkout belongs to its external provider; this website does not process payments.

YouTube connects only after you choose to play a YouTube video or open a YouTube link. YouTube, Facebook and payment providers use their own privacy policies. The church introduction, images and fonts are hosted with this website.
